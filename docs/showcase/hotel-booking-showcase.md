# Hotel Booking Showcase

`showcase/hotel_booking_showcase.py` is a luxury hospitality landing reference in Faststrap.

It demonstrates editorial product marketing, premium brand expression, and interactive booking flows.

## Gallery

![Hotel Booking Showcase](../assets/showcase/hotel-booking-showcase-home.png)

## What It Shows

- `Cormorant Garamond` display font + `Inter` body
- gold accent (#C9A84C) on dark navy (#0A0F1E) palette
- animated hero with floating "Award Winner" badge and live demand counter
- amenities glassmorphism strip: WiFi, Breakfast, Parking, Pool, Spa
- room cards with star ratings, amenity mini-badges, gold price highlight
- `AutoRefresh` live traveler count
- `ActiveSearch` room filtering
- `LazyLoad` testimonials and special offers
- `LoadingButton` availability check with toast alert
- `FooterModern`, `PageMeta`, `Fx` effects throughout

## Why It Matters

Hotel Booking Showcase is useful for teams evaluating whether Faststrap can support:

- luxury and hospitality landing pages
- editorial product marketing with premium visuals
- interactive booking and availability flows
- dark-mode-first brand expressions
