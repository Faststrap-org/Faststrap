# Agency Portfolio

`showcase/agency_portfolio.py` is the flagship creative/portfolio reference in Faststrap.

It is designed to show that the framework can support presentation-heavy, brand-led agency sites with modern interaction patterns.

## Gallery

![Agency Portfolio showcase](../assets/showcase/agency-portfolio-home.png)

## What It Shows

- a stronger editorial and presentation-led visual direction
- custom polish layered over Bootstrap-native structure
- modern navigation, testimonial, and CTA composition
- a more expressive page rhythm than the earlier example-tier landing pages
- `InfiniteScroll` project gallery with rich mock UI previews
- `GlassNavbar`, `TestimonialSection`, and `LoadingButton`
- `ThemeToggle` with light/dark support

## Why It Matters

Agency Portfolio is the clearest showcase of Faststrap’s portfolio/agency ceiling. It is useful for teams evaluating whether the framework can support:

- brand-forward creative sites
- portfolio and case-study layouts
- modern agency and studio presentations
- HTMX-first interactions without a JavaScript-heavy stack
