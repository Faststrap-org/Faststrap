# LearnLoop Academy

`showcase/learnloop_academy.py` is an education and progress-driven product reference in Faststrap.

It demonstrates course and progress-oriented product interfaces with an energetic visual system.

## Gallery

![LearnLoop Academy showcase](../assets/showcase/learnloop-academy-home.png)

## What It Shows

- `Inter` body + `Syne` display headlines
- electric violet / teal dual-tone dark palette
- `DashboardLayout`-style shell with progress tracking sidebar
- responsive grids throughout
- `Progress` bars, `Tabs`, `Accordion` for course content patterns
- `Fx` entrance + hover animations on every section
- HTMX `AutoRefresh` for live enrollment counter

## Why It Matters

LearnLoop Academy is useful for teams evaluating whether Faststrap can support:

- edtech and online course platforms
- progress and achievement-driven UIs
- energetic, youth-focused product interfaces
- dashboard-style learning shells
