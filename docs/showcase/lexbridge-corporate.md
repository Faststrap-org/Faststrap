# Lexbridge Corporate

`showcase/lexbridge_corporate.py` is a corporate/professional brand reference in Faststrap.

It demonstrates a restrained brand style suited to consulting, legal, and professional-service sites.

## Gallery

![Lexbridge Corporate showcase](../assets/showcase/lexbridge-corporate-home.png)

## What It Shows

- `Cormorant Garamond` display font + `Inter` body
- slate/gold professional light-mode palette
- atmospheric hero with radial gradient overlay
- `Accordion` for practice areas / FAQs
- trust indicators: stat banner, team cards, client logos
- `Fx` entrance + hover animations on every section

## Why It Matters

Lexbridge Corporate is useful for teams evaluating whether Faststrap can support:

- law firm and consulting websites
- professional service and corporate presences
- restrained, trust-forward brand expressions
- accordion-heavy FAQ and service sections
