# Carenest Clinic

`showcase/carenest_clinic.py` is a healthcare-focused product surface in Faststrap.

It demonstrates trust-heavy layout, appointment flows, and theme-aware styling in a medical context.

## Gallery

![Carenest Clinic in light mode](../assets/showcase/carenest-clinic-light.png)

![Carenest Clinic in dark mode](../assets/showcase/carenest-clinic-dark.png)

## What It Shows

- `AuthLayout` for login and registration flows
- `FormBuilder`, `FormErrorSummary`, `FloatingLabel`, `InputGroup` for full form coverage
- `Modal` for appointment confirmation, `NoticeAlert` for status feedback
- `Switch`, `Checkbox` for patient preference forms
- `ThemeToggle` for dual light/dark support
- HTMX-first interactions (form submission, search, slot availability)
- Custom CSS for medical-grade clean aesthetic (light-first, teal/slate brand)

## Why It Matters

Carenest Clinic is useful for teams evaluating whether Faststrap can support:

- healthcare and clinic appointment interfaces
- trust-heavy consumer health products
- form-intensive workflows with validation
- accessible, light-first design systems
