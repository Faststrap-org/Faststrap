# Furniture Store Showcase

`showcase/furniture_store_showcase.py` is a warm e-commerce/product storytelling reference in Faststrap.

It demonstrates editorial merchandising, product cards, testimonials, and trust sections with a premium brand feel.

## Gallery

![Furniture Store Showcase](../assets/showcase/furniture-store-showcase-home.png)

## What It Shows

- `Playfair Display` display font + `Inter` body
- warm cream palette (#FAF7F2) with walnut brown accents
- product cards: image zoom on hover, heart wishlist `Icon`, quick-view overlay
- `Why Choose Us`: `Icon()` components (patch-check, tree, house-heart, truck)
- stats banner: Orders / Homes / Cities / Years
- `TestimonialSection` with star ratings
- brands strip and news grid
- `FooterModern`, `PageMeta`, `Fx` animations throughout

## Why It Matters

Furniture Store Showcase is useful for teams evaluating whether Faststrap can support:

- product storytelling and editorial e-commerce
- warm, brand-forward retail sites
- trust and social proof sections
- premium furniture and home goods marketing
